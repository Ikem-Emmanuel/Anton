## Brand Voice

- Tone: Professional but approachable, confident not arrogant
- Active voice, present tense, "you" to address the reader
- Short sentences (avg 15-20 words), no jargon without explanation
- Full guidelines: brand-guide.md (create when ready)

## Content Standards

- Blog posts: 1500-2500 words, Flesch reading ease 60-70
- Landing pages: Clear value prop above fold, single CTA per section
- Email: Subject lines <50 chars, preview <90 chars, mobile-first
- Meta description: 150-155 chars with primary keyword
- Title tag: 55-60 chars, keyword near front

## Approval Workflow

1. Writer drafts to content/drafts/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md
2. Content SEO reviews for keyword optimization
3. CMO reviews for brand voice and strategy
4. Approved → content/published/
